## Standard references used for the correctness pass
The Quran (verse references are given in explanations, e.g. 4:12, 9:60, 31:13, 33:40), Sahih al-Bukhari/Muslim narrations (cited in explanations), Ibn Ishaq/Ibn Hisham Seerah chronology, and well-known dates of the Rashidun, Umayyad, Abbasid and Ottoman periods.
