Other facts were checked against standard references (Wikipedia, the Constitution of Pakistan, and standard Pakistan Studies texts).

Raw HTML, the parsers (`parse_cp.py`, `parse_pj.py`), the per-item decisions (`cp_decisions.py`, `pj_decisions.py`), the build script (`build.py`) and per-question metadata (`build_meta.json`) are in `raw/`.
